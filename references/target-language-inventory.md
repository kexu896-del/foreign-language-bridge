# 功能-情景目标清单（v1）

> 用途：给预习卡和覆盖率台账提供**可复用的目标语言**，减少对模型临场生成的依赖。
> 用法：每次只选 2–3 个核心功能 + 1 个迁移目标；不要求一次用出全部。
> 边界：不是语法大纲，不构成等级认证；v1 草稿，按 Track A 的真实缺口增补。

## 一、核心功能与分级表达

| 功能 | 入门（A2–B1） | 中级（B1–B2） | 进阶（B2–C1） |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 开场/说明目的 | `I'd like to talk about...`；`We need to discuss...` | `The purpose of today's call is to...`；`I'd like to align on...` | `I'd like to align on scope, timeline, and next steps.`；`The main decision we need today is...` |
| 2 澄清/确认 | `What do you mean by...?`；`Do you mean A or B?` | `Just to make sure we're on the same page, do you mean...?`；`So you're saying...?` | `Could you clarify whether you're asking about the cause or the next step?`；`Let me check I've understood: ...` |
| 3 没听清/修复 | `Sorry, could you say that again?`；`Please speak more slowly.` | `Sorry, I didn't catch the last part.`；`Could you repeat the figure?` | `I missed the number—was it fifteen or fifty?`；`Let me put that another way.` |
| 4 不确定/边界 | `I'm not sure. I'll check.`；`I can't say now.` | `I can't confirm that yet; I'll get back to you by Friday.`；`I don't have that information yet.` | `I can't commit to that today; the earliest I can confirm is after the review.`；`That's outside what I'm authorized to agree.` |
| 5 范围/排除 | `This includes A and B. It doesn't include C.`；`C is not in this project.` | `The first release covers A and B; C is out of scope for now.`；`Let's keep C for a later phase.` | `To be clear on scope, A and B are in; C is a separate phase with its own sign-off.`；`What's not included is...` |
| 6 依赖/条件 | `We need your approval first.`；`If you are late, we are late.` | `Our timeline depends on your security review.`；`If the review slips, we'll need a fallback.` | `If the review slips, the June date is at risk; as a fallback, we could phase the rollout.`；`This is conditional on...` |
| 7 建议/协商/fallback | `Can we do X?`；`Maybe we can start with A.` | `What if we start with A and add B later?`；`Could we agree on a smaller first release?` | `As a fallback, we could phase the rollout and keep the June date for the core module.`；`Would it make sense to trade X for Y?` |
| 8 拒绝/不同意 | `Sorry, I can't do that.`；`I don't agree.` | `I understand, but we can't accept that timeline.`；`That doesn't work for us because...` | `I see your point, but we can't take on unlimited liability; what we can offer is a cap at X.`；`We'd rather not sign that as drafted.` |
| 9 追问/推进 | `Why?`；`What happens next?` | `What's the main reason for that?`；`Who owns this on your side?` | `What would need to change for you to accept this?`；`What's your main concern here?` |
| 10 总结/行动项 | `So we do A today and B tomorrow.`；`You will call me.` | `Let me summarize: A is confirmed, B is pending, and C owns the next step.`；`I'll send a recap with owners and dates.` | `To recap: we'll pilot on Monday, review on Wednesday, and decide on Friday; the owner is C.`；`Let's confirm the action items before we close.` |
| 11 电话/服务 | `I'm calling about my order.`；`Can you help me?` | `I'm calling to check the status of my order.`；`Could you confirm the delivery date?` | `I'd like to confirm the delivery date and get that in writing.`；`Could you escalate this and give me a reference number?` |
| 12 寒暄/结束 | `Nice to meet you. Bye.`；`How are you?` | `It was great talking to you; let's follow up next week.`；`How's your week going?` | `I enjoyed the conversation; I'll send a short note with the next step.`；`Let's park the rest and pick it up next week.` |

## 二、情景映射（12 个情景卡）

| 情景 | 核心功能 | 本场目标（示例） | 迁移目标 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W1 项目启动 | 1 开场、5 范围、6 依赖、9 追问、10 总结 | purpose；what's not included；depends on；who owns；recap | 用 `What's not included...` 主动划界 |
| W2 交付延迟 | 6 依赖、7 协商、8 拒绝、10 总结 | impact；can't accept；fallback；confirm by | 给一个可接受替代方案 |
| W3 内部简报 | 1 目的、4 边界、8 不同意、10 下一步 | key point；based on；can't guarantee；recommend | 结论先行，再给限制 |
| W4 谈判要点 | 2 澄清、7 协商、8 拒绝、10 总结 | read the same way；main concern；what we can offer；recap | 把“不能接受”转成具体条款 |
| W5 英文面试 | 1 开场、9 追问、10 结果、4 边界 | my role；what I did；as a result；I didn't own that part | 用 STAR 说清结果与边界 |
| W6 行业社交 | 1 开场、9 追问、12 结束 | I work on；how about you；great talking to you | 30 秒介绍 + 一个反问 |
| D1 寒暄 | 12 寒暄、9 追问、12 结束 | how's your week；what's keeping you busy | 自然接话并回问 |
| D2 电话/客服 | 11 电话、3 修复、2 确认、10 确认 | calling about；didn't catch；could you confirm；in writing | 请求书面确认 |
| D3 出行/机场 | 11 服务、2 确认、7 替代 | connecting to；what's the latest；alternative | 问清时间与替代方案 |
| D4 社交/晚餐 | 12 寒暄、8 不同意、9 追问 | I'm not sure about that；what do you think | 礼貌表达不同意见 |
| D5 酒店 | 11 服务、5 范围、7 替代、10 确认 | reservation under；mix-up；alternative；in writing | 说清差异并拿到解决方案 |
| D6 餐厅/咖啡 | 11 服务、2 确认、8 问题、10 结账 | could I get；without；there's been a mistake；split the bill | 礼貌纠错并确认订单 |

## 三、选择规则

1. 每场从“核心功能”里选 2–3 个，从情景映射里选 3–5 个目标表达；其余作为识认词库，不强制用出。
2. 迁移目标只选 1 个，放在会话后 2–5 分钟的 drill 里；不要在同一场塞两个新功能。
3. 预习卡的关键词可以超出本清单（主题词），但**主动目标**必须来自本清单或本场情景卡。
4. 覆盖率台账按目标逐项记 `modeled / intent_prompted / keyword_prompted / independent / correct / retest`；示范不算掌握。
5. 连续两次 `needs_review` 的功能，下一场优先复测；不要同时开三个新功能。
6. 如果 Track A 反复出现清单外的表达，先记为候选，按“是否高频、是否可迁移、是否影响任务”决定是否加入 v1.1。

## 四、未覆盖（基础版之外）

- 正式演讲、主持、辩论、复杂谈判策略、法律意见口头表达、跨文化冲突调解；
- 发音/流利度的系统训练与认证；
- 语法体系与大规模词汇记忆。

这些不在本清单承诺范围内；需要时另建专项，不把练习记录写成能力认证。
